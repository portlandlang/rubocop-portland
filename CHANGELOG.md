- **Seven more report-only cops**, by method or constant name: `Portland/MethodMissing`, `Portland/RuntimeDefineMethod` (`define_method`, `const_get`, `const_set`), `Portland/EvalFamily` (`eval`, `send`, `public_send`, `instance_eval`, `instance_variable_get`, and kin), `Portland/Fetch`, `Portland/InPlaceMutator` (`push`, `upcase!`, and the rest of the mutators; receiver-blind, as the census is), `Portland/FreezeFamily` (`freeze`, `frozen?`, `dup`, `clone`), and `Portland/ThreadModel` (`Thread`, `Mutex`, `Queue`, `Fiber`, `Ractor`, top-level only). With the first eight, every decided removal the readiness census counts has a cop.

- **Seven report-only cops** for the decided Ruby differences the readiness census counts, each naming what Portland says and where the value or behavior goes instead: `Portland/GlobalVariable` (`$count`, `$1`, `$~`), `Portland/BeginEndBlock`, `Portland/FlipFlop`, `Portland/ClassVariable` (`@@total`), `Portland/Inheritance` (`class Token < Node` and `super`), `Portland/SingletonClass` (`class << self`), and `Portland/RaiseRescue` (`raise`, `fail`, `rescue`, `ensure`, `retry`). Each carries its `Difference` and `Status` in `config/default.yml`. Specs parse as Ruby 4.0. The gem's own lint turns the Portland department off, since its source is Ruby, not code on its way to Portland.

- **`Portland/ForLoop`**, the first cop: `for word in words` refuses in Portland (docs/ruby/removed-syntax.md), and the cop rewrites its head to `words.each do |word|`, taking an optional `do`, keeping several loop variables as block parameters, and parenthesizing a range or an operator expression before `.each`. The body and `end` are untouched. The rewrite is marked unsafe, since a `for` variable outlives its loop and a block parameter doesn't. `config/default.yml` gives each cop, beyond RuboCop's keys, the `Difference` (thesis, taste, or gap) and `Status` (decided or undecided) that ruby_research's readiness census reads.
